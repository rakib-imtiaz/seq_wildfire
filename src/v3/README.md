# v3 "Maquette"

The main page since 2026-10-21: served at `/` (`index.html` → `src/v3/main.tsx`; it was `/v3/`). The form stays at `/proposal` (the v1 app) and the earlier design is at `/v1/`. Plan: `docs/v3-plan.md`. Shared rules: `docs/versions-brief.md`.

## Dials (taste-skill §1)
- `DESIGN_VARIANCE 6`: asymmetric but calm; an architect's presentation board, not an agency site.
- `MOTION_INTENSITY 6`: one authored moment (the pinned maquette), quiet reveals elsewhere.
- `VISUAL_DENSITY 4`: airy, but each beat carries a short fact sheet (the user: "the side text too minimal").

## Deliberate overrides of the taste skill
- **Light only, no dark mode.** The plan locks a light studio; the whole concept is daylight on a scale model.
- **No icon library.** The brief forbids installing anything, so the 4 glyphs (arrows, play, pause) are authored SVG, one 1.5 px stroke.
- **Google Fonts `<link>`.** The brief mandates it (shared rule).
- **Hand-written CSS, no Tailwind.** make_3d_website: "the CSS is part of the craft"; Tailwind is not installed.

## The 3D (one canvas, one WebGL context)
- **One scale for everything (2026-10-20 rework).** The user's note: "the physics, the object shape, nothing is matching". The first build put the real-size M3T over a 1:250 model, so the drone read as an 87 m aircraft, twice its own thermal footprint, and the stepped boards threw the footprints onto vertical walls. Now the land, the infrastructure, the forest and the drone are ALL 1:250, so every size, height and footprint is true to the others.
- **Not cartoony (2026-10-21).** The user, three times: "the frame looks soo cartoony the tree models also looks cartoony", "too cartoony". The white plaster land with smooth white cone trees read as a toy up close. Now the land is painted (dry open ground, forest floor under the stands, rock on steep faces, gravel roads, matte slate water), the trees are the v1 table's four species, the pylons are lattice steel and the buildings concrete. The cut sides and the base stay white (a painted terrain model on a white base). The v1 rule holds here too: procedural trees look toy-like close up, so the coverage and heat beats frame from further out (`fill` 0.5 to 0.64).
- **Model:** the shared fictional watch zone (`/table/js/zone.js`, 1600 × 1000 m) at 1:250 (6.4 × 4.0 m of "studio") as one milled surface from the shared height field (`src/lib/terrain.ts`), with contour lines engraved by the shader (10 m, index 50 m; faint on the model, inked in for the map). The reservoir is a basin at 30 m (14 m under the dam crest) with a low bank on the designed shoreline.
- **Forest:** 22k instanced conifers (30k on `high`): Douglas-fir, spruce (cooler north-facing slopes), lodgepole (a few rust and grey snags) and ponderosa (dry south-facing benches). Star-sectioned tiers, per-tree tint and stand-scale colour patches, random placement over the forest density (no planted grid). The SAMPLE hotspot is a smouldering stump in a small canopy gap, as in the report's imagery. Trees carry the lens footprints and the thermal like the ground does.
- **Drone:** the real M3T build (`/table/js/m3t/model.js`, metres) scaled by 1:250. It hovers 77 m out from the ~37° slope along its normal (`optics.js` STANDOFF), which is 100 m above the ground under it, heading and gimbal squared to the slope. Rendered in a second pass (layer 1) with its own shadow-casting copy of the key light, framed on the 0.4 m aircraft. Close beats are macro shots of it; from the coverage beat on it is a point at the apex of its own field of view, marked by a quadcopter map symbol (`Labels.tsx`, not to scale) and an "M3T, 100 m above ground" plate.
- **Optics:** each lens is a real perspective camera with FACTS §3 FOVs (wide 84°, tele 15°, thermal 61° DFOV, read as diagonal; 4:3, 4:3, 5:4). Its frustum is projected onto the surface, the trees and the massing in the shader. W / T / IR toggles isolate one cone. The coverage beat's footprint figures (111 × 83, 16 × 12, 71 × 57 m) come from the same optics and STANDOFF (`data/copy.ts`).
- **Heat (2026-10-21):** the land is never painted with ironbow (a magenta sheet over the trees read as "so un professional"). The heat beat shows what the thermal lens sees as an inset: the engine renders the land from the lens's own camera with the thermal materials (`kit.thermal`: cool crowns, warmer ground, cold water, the 312 °C core) into a target and composites it top-right; `components/ThermalFrame.tsx` draws its frame (lens, Iron Red, low gain, a spot meter, the palette scale). On the model the footprint is a cased cobalt outline over the canopy (`OUTLINE_IR`).
- **Professional pass (2026-10-21):** the main site's type system (Barlow Condensed caps for headings and buttons, Barlow, JetBrains Mono for labels and data), square buttons, 3 px radii; a chapter number over each beat; titles without full stops. Cut sides show strata (topsoil, subsoil, till, rock beds, water in section). Cut blocks are irregular polygons with young regrowth. Routes have a pale casing and show only in the team and map beats (`ROUTES`); the team beat looks from the east, down both routes.
- **Camera:** each beat frames a box; across a big zoom the frame's centre travels with its real size, so the aircraft stays in view through the pull-back. Haze (fog to the studio colour) in the close beats only.
- **Side copy:** every beat has a fact sheet under its line (`components/BeatFacts.tsx`, all from FACTS.md). Phones under 960 px tall hide it in the pinned story (the picture stays big); the motion-off story shows it everywhere.
- **Beats** = f(P), P ∈ 0..6: hero, lenses, coverage, team, heat, map, report. Continuous motion (props, bob, slow orbit) runs on a separate clock.
- **Closing shot (report beat).** The user: "the ending frame should be something good looking" (it was the top-down map with the report card over it). Now the camera pulls back from the map to the whole model on its base, three-quarter from the south-west, a slow orbit, the thermal cone and a "SAMPLE 312 °C" plate on the hotspot. The report card is a landscape sheet under the model; Story measures it and `setReserve()` keeps the model framed clear of it (phones: a compact three-row sheet). The motion-off story renders the same shot (`renderStill(…, { full: true })`) with the card under it.
- **Assets named (2026-10-21).** The user asked "whats the role of this??" (the substation) and "whats this??" (the dam and river). The overviews name the site's assets with quiet mono plates: all six in the team beat (reservoir, dam, powerhouse, access road, transmission line, substation), the line and substation on the map, four on the whole model. The switchyard and substation are fenced gravel yards with steel transformers and gantries. The SAMPLE tag shows from the team beat on.
- **Water and the aerial route (2026-10-21).** The user: the water and "the arila marker" didn't look good. Water colour comes from its depth (the surface's height texture, `tex.height`): a pale wet rim, shallow teal, deep blue-black, with a broad ripple in the normal. The aerial route is cobalt dashes with a faint ground track under it, and the drone is a cobalt position dot in a halo. "Launch" is named in the team beat.
- **Sections (2026-10-21, "redesign the other sections").** Each has a mono label on a cobalt rule. Services is an even 3-column grid (the heading in the first cell, five numbered cards, one 4:3 crop). Applications is top-aligned with a numbered site list and a square compare handle. Why is the contrast line, then the key point beside the five numbered aims, with the commitment as a labelled row under them.
- **Motion for text (2026-10-21, "animate this, make it cool, use the text animation all over").** `components/BrandMark.tsx`: the header wordmark's strokes draw in letter by letter, its bars extend, a cobalt scan passes over it, and the descriptor decodes; hover replays it (the shared `Wordmark` now carries `--i` and `pathLength=1`, with no visual change on v1). `components/TextMotion.tsx`: `SplitText` (headings rise word by word out of a mask, run by CSS on `[data-in]`, `.beat[data-on]` or `.st-play`) and `Scramble` (mono labels and numbers decode in view, or when a beat turns active). Motion off: all static.
- **Thermal beat focus (2026-10-21).** The land outside the thermal footprint pales toward the studio (`uFocus`, heat beat only), and a cased cobalt connector runs from under the inset's reading to the hotspot on the model (the user drew it).
- **Services as an index.** One row per service: number, title, line, a small desaturated photo (full colour on hover).
- **Careers (2026-10-21).** The client's recruiting post (the user's PDF, FACTS §6) as `sections/Careers.tsx` after the closing band: pitch and photo, three numbered lists, a terms strip, "Email your resume" to career@sequoiaservices.ca (a mailto link; nothing is sent by the site). A footer link points to it.
- **Header navigation (2026-10-21, "nothing in the header").** Section links (How it works, Services, Applications, Why Sequoia, Careers) in condensed caps, the one in view underlined in cobalt. Under 1100 px a Menu button opens a full-screen sheet (numbered links, the proposal button, the phone; scroll locked, Escape closes). Phones drop the header button (it is in the sheet).
- **Flowing water (2026-10-21).** The river carries its downstream direction and its distance from the dam (`aFlow`); the water shader moves its ripples and flow streaks with the current on the engine clock (`uTime`), adds froth along the banks and whitewater below the spillway. The reservoir's ripples drift on a light wind.
- **Text economy (2026-10-21, "reduce the text all over, dont make it text heavy").** One short line per beat, at most three spec rows, no notes; the report card has three rows and no "Shared with"; Why has three aims; Careers is a job listing with two items per column (the contract-awards line removed at the user's request); a shorter footer note. No em dashes anywhere on the served pages (checked in the rendered text).
- **Footer (2026-10-21, the user's comp).** Near-black with ember (#e8782c) accents only here: the outlined, glowing "Request a proposal" link, the ember segment on the rule, the pin and phone icons, the ↗. Three ruled columns. `components/FooterTopo.tsx` draws a live topographic corner (16 closed contour rings around an off-corner summit, every fifth an index line); the hill breathes and turns slowly and a fire front runs along one lit contour. Motion off: a still map.
- **One CTA on screen.** The header's "Request a proposal" is always visible, so the story beats carry none (the user: "lasty frame two same buttons").
- **Wordmark:** the header and footer use the main site's `Wordmark` / `Logo` (`src/components/Logo.tsx`, the client's card); phones under 520 px drop the descriptor line so it never runs under the button.
- **Review hooks:** `?beat=0..6`, `?frame=<P>` (synchronous still, scroll driver off), `?lens=wide|tele|thermal`, `?q=lite|balanced|high`, `?motion=0`. `window.__v3` exposes `seek(P)`, `stats()` (incl. `agl`, `trees`, `waterLevel`), `engine`.
- **Static path** (`?motion=0`, reduced motion): one offscreen context renders a still per beat from the same scene, the same labels are laid over them, then the page is a stacked article. `?q=lite` / no WebGL: real photos instead.
- **Canvas role:** `aria-hidden`; every beat's heading, line and facts are in the DOM beside it.
